At the AI Tinkerers Global Hackathon: Agents Everywhere in Islamabad, our team built **Kaamyaabi**, an AI agent focused on meeting workers where they already are, on WhatsApp, instead of asking them to adopt a new platform.

The theme of the hackathon was building AI agents that integrate into existing communication channels rather than requiring new apps or interfaces.

## What We Built

Kaamyaabi lets workers in informal labor markets send a single WhatsApp voice note in Urdu or Punjabi. From that one voice note, the agent:

- Builds a user profile
- Writes a CV
- Finds matching jobs
- Negotiates the offer

All of this happens in about 19 seconds, while the worker is still on the road.
